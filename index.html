How CivicReport Platform Works

A simple process that connects citizens with the appropriate authorities to solve community problems.

🖊

1. Report

Citizens submit reports about potholes, broken infrastructure, waste, security concerns and other community issues.

👁‍🗨

2. Track

Users can follow the status of submitted reports and receive updates as authorities review and process the issue.

☑

Resolve

Authorities investigate reported problems, take appropriate action and update the community when issues are resolved.
