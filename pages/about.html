Our Mission

CivicReport was built to give every citizen a simple way to report community problems such as vandalism, road hazards, noise, and suspicious activities — and to help authorities respond faster with clear information.

What is CivicReport?

CivicReport is a community incident reporting platform. Citizens can submit reports with photos, location, and contact details. Authorities and admins can view, filter, and update the status of each report from dedicated dashboards.

The system runs fully in the browser using local storage, making it easy to use without complex setup — ideal for estates, schools, and local communities.

Key Features

  • Report incidents with name, phone, category, location & photo
  • Public Community Feed with search and filters
  • User Dashboard with personal stats and recent reports
  • Authority Dashboard for actionable items and status updates
  • Admin System Overview with full table, CSV/JSON export
  • Live incident map and priority markers
  • Safety Tools Marketplace (fire extinguishers & more)

Who is it for?

CivicReport is designed for ordinary citizens, estate managers, local authorities, security teams, schools, and community leaders who want a simple way to track and resolve local issues.
